Work From Home Policy

Effective 1 Oct 2026 · Owner: HR · 4 min read
What changed from v2 WFH days per week raised from 2 to 3. Core collaboration hours now 11:00 – 16:00 IST.

1. Eligibility

All confirmed employees may work from home up to three days a week, with their manager's agreement. Employees on probation need HR approval.

2. Availability

Be reachable on chat and calls during core hours, and keep your calendar up to date.

3. Equipment & security

Use company laptops only, connect over VPN, and never work from public Wi-Fi without it.
